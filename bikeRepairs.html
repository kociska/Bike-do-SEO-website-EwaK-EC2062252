About Us

Bike King is a locally owned bicycle shop based in Kelso, serving riders across the Scottish Borders. Whether you're cycling the Tweed Valley trails or commuting through town, we offer quality bikes, expert repairs, and friendly service to keep you moving.

Our Mission

At Bike King, our mission is to make cycling in the Scottish Borders accessible, enjoyable, and reliable for everyone. From the rolling hills around Kelso to the scenic Borderloop and Tweed Cycle Route, we’re here to support your ride with expert service, quality bikes, and a passion for the outdoors.

Our Vision

The company are unique in the Borders due to their location and having the benefit of brilliant cycle trails on their doorstep. They pride themselves in selling only quality products and guaranteeing servicing and repair.
